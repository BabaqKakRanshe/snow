## Giant skeleton (Models/Giant_Skeleton.fbx, Textures/T_Giant_A.jpg, T_Giant_M.png)

Bone geometry: **BodyParts3D**, © The Database Center for Life Science (DBCLS), licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Source: BodyParts3D 4.0 (Life Science Database Archive,
https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/).

Changes: 150 bones merged and decimated (~97k triangles), refitted and rescaled to the game's giant rig, new UV layout and
baked textures; the backs of both eye sockets of the skull are opened into the brain cavity.

CC BY 4.0 requires the attribution above in the game's credits (or another place players can see), with a note that the
data was modified.
